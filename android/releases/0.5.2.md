Tracker files: add private supporting documents directly from the Files tab, or attach them while posting a progress update. File-only uploads leave progress unchanged. Files show their uploader, date, size and note, and older files can be loaded even when their updates are outside the recent history window.

Uploads support up to five PDF, image, text, Word or Excel files per update, up to 10 MB each. Select multiple files, remove unwanted selections before posting, and retry failed submissions without duplicate updates. Files remain private to conversation members; item-only shares do not expose chat evidence. The matching ERP backend update is required.
