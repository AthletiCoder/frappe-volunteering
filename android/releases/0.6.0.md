SF Tasks 0.6.0 adds:

- Light, Dark and System appearance, with a readable dark palette and distinct work-state colours.
- Subtasks with a responsible person, deadline and Open / In progress / Completed status. Complete the subtasks before completing the parent task.
- A Comments tab on each tracker. Conversation members can discuss that tracker without changing progress; viewers can read.
- @mentions selected from conversation members, plus a My mentions inbox with unread filtering and links back to context.
- Encrypted drafts and an Outbox for messages, tasks, progress, comments and tracker files. Waiting work retries when connected. Rejected or conflicting changes need review rather than silently overwriting someone else's work.
- Search across older messages, tasks, trackers and private filenames, with category filters and direct opening.
- Notification preferences for all messages, mentions/assignments, mentions only or mute; per-chat overrides, quiet hours and reminder controls.

The matching ERP backend and schema migration are required for the new server features. App release publication does not deploy that backend. The web portal's light-theme policy is unchanged.
