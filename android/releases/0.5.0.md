SF Tasks now offers updates inside the app. Open the menu and choose Check for updates. New releases can be downloaded and installed after Android's confirmation, with your login and app data retained.

Trackers now show who needs to act next, a pending-with-me count, and red highlighting for your pending work. Chat progress updates display the tracker title, progress bar, current stage, responsible person and update note in that order. New trackers default to Status and let you enter your own stage names.

SF testing remains a separate local-only app with its own update feed.
