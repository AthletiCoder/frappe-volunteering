# SF Tasks 0.6.9

- Work logs: monthly calendars, compact hour/minute entry, and reporting-manager team review.
- Time and leave: employee requests, remaining paid and compensatory leave, HR policy controls, holidays and payroll guidance.
- People centre Guide: policy headings and bullet points, with organisation and work-location sections removed.
- Trackers: add and edit stages between existing stages without losing the current workflow position.

The work-log and time/leave services require the matching ERP backend changes. Building or installing this APK does not deploy the backend.
