Chat notifications: adds private, session-bound Firebase Cloud Messaging support. Alerts open the correct conversation, exclude the sender and already-read messages, and suppress alerts for the conversation currently on screen. Opening an alerted conversation directly from the chat list also clears its existing alert. Activation needs the matching ERP update and one-time Firebase project configuration.

Reminders: simultaneous due tasks and trackers now appear in one summary rather than a stack of separate notifications. Old reminder cards and alarms are cleared during the transition; distinct tasks remain individually listed within the summary.

Trackers: a new dashboard shows totals, your actions, completed work, search and filters, with trackers grouped by conversation. The tracker detail view now has Workflow, Details, Updates and Files tabs, a stage timeline and the relevant progress/share/configuration actions.
