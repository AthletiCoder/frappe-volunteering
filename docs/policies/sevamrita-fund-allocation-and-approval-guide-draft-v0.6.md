# Sevamrita Fund Allocation and Approval Guide

Draft for review | Version 0.6 | 12 October 2026

Every fund flow needs three clear decisions: **who allocates money, who approves its use, and who validates the bills.** This guide links those responsibilities to budgeting, advances, expenses and monthly reporting.

## 1 Who allocates approves and validates

<!-- GOVERNANCE DIAGRAM -->

**Financial Board members:** HG Priya Gopesh Prabhu and HG Kanai Thakur Prabhu.

**Spending approval** confirms the purpose, reasonableness and budget. **Accounts validation** checks bill standards and validity, then Accounts records and reconciles the transaction. One review does not replace the other.

**Proposed approval route:** Requests go directly to the campaign in-charge for operations or the project in-charge or representative for projects. Ratify the authority limits, an independent approver for their own requests, and whether this replaces or coexists with employee-grade routing. Existing portal routing continues until the change is approved and configured.

## 2 Allocate receipts as money comes in

Alongside the annual budget, review incoming Benevity funds and campaign donations as they are received. Maintain a dated allocation record showing the receipt base, operations share, project share, authority and period; quarterly releases remain separate decisions.

**Formula proposed for discussion:** Retain the lower of **30% of the agreed receipt base** and **INR 10 lakh** for operations; allocate the remainder to project funds under FB oversight. Compare the retained amount with the next three months' fixed costs and campaign needs. Check funding conditions before allocation.

<!-- ALLOCATION DIAGRAM -->

**Illustration only.** With INR 50 lakh as the agreed base, 30% is INR 15 lakh. The INR 10 lakh cap gives operations INR 10 lakh and projects INR 40 lakh. Before adoption, confirm which receipts qualify and whether the cap applies monthly, quarterly, annually or per release. An allocation authorisation does not itself transfer money or record expenditure.

# Run the cycle and keep it moving

<!-- CYCLE DIAGRAM -->

## 3 Plan create and release

**Center or project leader:** Prepare activities, outcomes, the annual budget and quarterly cash needs. Centers discuss the annual plan with HG Priya Gopesh Pr. Obtain the designated budget approval, then have the assigned Projects Manager approve the project setup, members and expense labels.

**EB for operations and FB for projects:** Oversee the relevant funding decisions. Accounts checks available money and previous utilisation. Further quarterly allocations require adequate bills and monthly reports supporting the preceding allocation.

## 4 Request spend and settle

**Employee:** Raise the advance using the portal **Purpose** field: activity, date, cost breakdown, total and money already held. Enter the required-by and expected settlement dates. The appropriate spending approver sanctions the request; Accounts arranges and records the actual payment.

Conduct the activity and submit proper bills against the correct project, activity and advance. Evidence is checked, the in-charge sanctions the cost under the proposed model, and Accounts finalises posting and settlement. Return unused funds and record any authorised retained balance. Do not claim a supplier bill already paid directly.

**Example:** INR 10,000 advanced, INR 8,000 accepted and posted, INR 2,000 actually returned: INR 0 advance outstanding and INR 8,000 expense. Pending bills remain separate. A new advance does not clear an older one.

## 5 Report review and repeat

**Center or project in-charge:** Ordinarily submit the monthly pack within **15 calendar days after month-end**. Include activity results, bills and claim references, budget versus actuals, advance and unpaid balances, open issues and the next month's cash needs. Submit on time with pending items disclosed.

**Report follow-up owner: Hemanth.** Maintain the due list, remind in-charges and escalate overdue reports to EB for operations or FB for projects. Accounts reconciles the balances; the relevant board and in-charge review results, assign actions and plan the next allocation. Carry unresolved items forward.

**Before reallocating between approved heads or activities, obtain Executive Board sanction.** Budget approval, fund allocation, advance payment and posted expense remain separate records. Confirm FB decision-making and delegated authority, the allocation formula and approval routing before adopting the revised governance model.
