# Sevamrita Foundation Accounting Policy

Draft for review | Version 0.1 | 9 October 2026

Policy owner: Accounts Manager. Approval authority: Board of Directors. Effective date: to be recorded after approval. Review frequency: annually and whenever a material legal or operating change occurs.

This policy governs how Sevamrita Foundation authorises spending, records income and expenditure, safeguards funds, and maintains evidence for audit. It applies to every office, project, employee, director and person handling the Foundation's money. It covers the ERPNext based Sevamrita portal, bank operations and supporting records kept outside the portal.

The central requirement is that every transaction has a business purpose, appropriate authority, sufficient evidence, the correct accounting date and classification, and a traceable settlement. A project approval, receipt verification, accounting entry and bank transfer each perform a different function.

**Review status.** This draft is submitted for management and Board review. The authority amounts in section 3 reflect the portal's seeded defaults and remain subject to ratification. New deadlines, procurement quotation requirements and other proposed operating rules are identified as proposals. Appendix A records the decisions needed before adoption. Approval of this document must be followed by a check that saved portal settings and operating procedures match the approved policy.

## 1 Accounting basis and fundamental rules

1.1 Sevamrita shall maintain accrual accounts using double entry bookkeeping. Its financial year runs from 1 April to 31 March and its reporting currency is Indian rupees. The applicable financial reporting framework and statutory presentation shall be confirmed by the Foundation's Chartered Accountant and approved with the annual accounting schedule. Section 8 status alone shall not be treated as proof of income tax exemption or donor deduction eligibility. [R1]

1.2 The General Ledger is the authoritative record of posted financial transactions. Source documents, party balances, bank statements and project reports shall reconcile to it. Budget Health measures commitments as well as posted claims and shall not be substituted for the statutory expenditure statement.

1.3 Recognition and settlement shall be recorded separately. An approved advance request is an authorisation; a disbursed advance is a recoverable asset. An expense accepted and posted by Accounts is expenditure and, where unpaid, a liability. Its later reimbursement clears that liability without recording the expense again.

1.4 For ordinary employee expenses, the verified invoice date shall be the claim posting date and shall determine its financial year. The submission date, approval date and payment date shall be preserved separately. An invoice that represents an asset, prepaid service, recoverable tax or another special accounting item shall receive the appropriate treatment under section 11; its date does not make the entire amount an immediate expense.

1.5 No person shall approve their own expense claim or advance, or independently verify their own receipts. No purchase, invoice or claim shall be artificially split to avoid an authority limit, budget control, procurement rule or cash restriction.

1.6 Posted records shall be corrected through authorised cancellation, amendment, reversal or adjustment documents. Financial records shall not be deleted or directly rewritten to conceal errors. Every correction shall retain its reason, supporting evidence, preparer, reviewer and links to the original records.

## 2 Responsibilities and separation of duties

The Board shall approve this policy, the delegation of financial authority, annual budgets, annual financial statements and material exceptions. It shall designate the people responsible for bank authorisation, financial oversight and statutory compliance. A grade that permits an unlimited amount in the portal remains subject to these governance duties, available funds and donor restrictions.

| Function | Responsibility | Boundary of authority |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Employee or claimant | Record the actual expense, funding source, project, invoice and business purpose; account for advances | Cannot choose final posting ledgers, approve their own claim or initiate payment through claim submission |
| Project owner | Plan activities and budgets; monitor commitments, delivery and funding conditions | Ownership or membership alone grants no spending approval or payment authority |
| Assigned Projects Manager | Decide project proposals and subsequent configuration changes | Project approval does not approve individual claims or permit receipt verification or payment |
| Expense Receipt Reviewer | Check evidence and invoice details; verify or request correction | Receipt verification does not sanction expenditure or post a payment |
| Reporting authority | Review necessity, reasonableness, eligibility and sanctioned amount; approve, reject or escalate | Must act within the relevant grade limit and cannot skip the reporting chain |
| Accounts Manager | Maintain accounting masters; map project labels; classify and finalise claims; approve employee bank details; supervise closing and compliance | Accounting responsibility does not replace the reporting authority's spending decision |
| Accounts User | Prepare and record authorised disbursements and reimbursements; maintain reconciliations within granted permissions | Cannot classify claims, decide project proposals or approve bank detail requests solely by holding this role |
| Auditor or independent finance reviewer | Inspect records and reconciliations; report exceptions | Shall not use audit access to originate or approve the transactions being reviewed |
| System Manager | Maintain technical access, configuration, backups and fiscal year setup | Technical access does not confer ordinary financial decision authority |

**Proposed operating control.** The claimant, receipt reviewer and spending approver shall be different people. Payment preparation and bank release shall also be separated where staffing permits. When Accounts classification and payment recording are performed by one person, a second authorised person shall review the supporting vouchers and bank reconciliation monthly. Overlapping roles shall be documented and reviewed by a Board designated director. Administrator access shall be restricted to exceptional technical work with a recorded reason.

## 3 Delegation of financial authority

3.1 Financial authority shall follow Employee Grade and the employee's Reports To chain. Designation records the job title; a portal role grants access. Neither is a substitute for the monetary authority schedule. Accounts shall ensure that employee records, active user links and reporting relationships are current before requests are submitted.

| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Associate | INR 0 | INR 0 |
| Manager | INR 2,000 | INR 2,000 |
| Vice President | INR 5,000 | INR 5,000 |
| President | INR 10,000 | INR 10,000 |
| Director | INR 25,000 | INR 25,000 |
| CEO | INR 50,000 | INR 50,000 |
| Executive Board | INR 1,00,000 | INR 1,00,000 |
| Board of Directors | Unlimited in the portal | Unlimited in the portal |

3.2 These are proposed policy limits based on seeded configuration, not confirmation of live saved values. Advance limits and expense limits are maintained separately. The Board shall ratify the final schedule and Accounts shall retain a dated settings export. A change to a limit requires recorded authorisation before the configuration is changed.

3.3 Each request shall first reach the immediate reporting manager. A manager whose limit is insufficient shall review and escalate to the next actual person in Reports To, recording a reason, or reject. An eligible person with sufficient authority may approve. The Board fallback shall be used if the chain ends; it shall not be used to bypass an available linked reviewer.

3.4 Expense approval uses the individual claim amount. For a linked multi invoice submission, authority uses at least the original combined submission amount, or the higher current combined amount following corrections. Creating separate dated accounting claims shall not reduce the required approval level. Other unrelated employee claims are not aggregated into advance exposure.

3.5 Advance approval uses the employee's live exposure: the current request plus other qualifying pending and approved advance commitments, reduced by recorded settlement and returns. Undisbursed approved portions also count. The portal's Home calculation excludes legacy advances outside the governed Home workflow; Accounts shall review any genuine legacy employee exposure separately before approval or disbursement.

3.6 A strict budget override requires the configured authorised person and a recorded Budget Exceedance Reason. Board of Directors grade has override authority in the portal; an additional configured role may also have it. Overrides shall remain within donor conditions and lawful use of funds. Ordinary monetary authority alone does not authorise a strict budget override.

## 4 Project approval and budget control

4.1 New projects and changes to their approved scope, owner, members, budget, cost centre or operating status shall be requested through the governed proposal process. The proposer shall identify an assigned Projects Manager. One decision by that assigned manager is sufficient in the current workflow. The manager may edit and approve, approve unchanged, return with comments or reject. Submitted requests and their decision histories shall be retained.

4.2 Spending shall relate to an approved Active project that is open for financial activity. Employees must be authorised members or otherwise eligible under the relevant portal service. Basic membership permits bill submission; Financial membership permits broader financial visibility. These visibility levels shall not be treated as different reimbursement entitlements.

4.3 Each project shall have one approved leaf Cost Centre. The ledger identifies what was purchased; the Project identifies the activity; the Cost Centre supplies the organisational reporting dimension. Departmental budgeting is not a prerequisite for project control in the Home workflow.

4.4 The approved project budget shall be divided into employee facing spending labels. Unallocated balance appears under Others. Accounts Managers shall map labels to one or more suggested expense ledgers after project approval. Missing suggestions shall not prevent a valid claim against an active approved label. Accounts shall determine the final ledger split before posting; a label does not become a posting account merely because it appears on a claim.

4.5 The overall project budget and expense category budget shall each have an explicit control mode: No Control, Warn Only or Strict. For Expense Claims, the category control follows the approved label. The mapping of several suggested ledgers to a label does not create several independent budget ceilings. Purchase Orders follow their configured account controls.

4.6 Submitted claims and Purchase Orders reserve budget before expenditure reaches the ledger. Draft, rejected and cancelled records are excluded by the relevant rules. Accounts finalised claims remain in the commitment total as posted expenditure. Employee advances do not consume project budget; their eventual claims do. A Purchase Invoice against a Purchase Order does not reserve the same budget a second time.

4.7 Project owners shall review remaining budget, pending commitments and posted costs together. Accounts shall separately reconcile project ledger expenditure, vendor invoices and historical journals because the commitment calculation principally follows Expense Claims and Purchase Orders.

**Proposed default.** Use Strict control for both overall and category budgets unless the project approval expressly selects another mode and explains why. Warn Only permits a decision with visible warnings; No Control requires management review outside automated ceiling enforcement. Completion or financial closure shall follow settlement of open claims, vendor commitments, advances and donor reporting. Projects with financial history shall be retained.

## 5 Procurement and spending routes

5.1 Planned purchases should ordinarily be made directly from the supplier through an approved Purchase Order and Purchase Invoice. An employee advance may fund approved operational needs. Reimbursement shall be used when the employee has actually paid personally and the expense is eligible. The selected route shall reflect the real transaction and shall not create a duplicate payment to a supplier or employee.

5.2 The portal's default vendor payment threshold is INR 5,000. Above the saved threshold, reimbursement requires a recorded reason to depart from the preferred vendor route, except for an identified emergency. This amount is an internal routing rule, not a legal procurement threshold. Accounts shall arrange any necessary correction before submission if a Home form does not expose the required reason field.

**Proposed quotation rule.** For planned purchases above INR 25,000, obtain three comparable quotations where practical. Record the basis of selection, including quality, availability and total cost. Sole source, specialist, time critical and donor nominated purchases require a written justification and approval at the applicable spending level. Donor conditions may require a stricter process. Consider the total requirement when applying thresholds.

5.3 The requester shall document the need and project. The reporting authority shall approve the commitment. The recipient shall confirm delivery, quantity and condition. Accounts shall match the approved order, delivery evidence and supplier invoice before posting or paying. Advances paid directly to suppliers shall be recorded against the approved order or appropriate vendor advance account and later adjusted against the invoice.

5.4 Purchase Invoices shall follow the approved Purchase Order chain required by the portal. The native accounting route shall be used by authorised Accounts staff when the required vendor or historical transaction is not available in Home. A limitation in the Home interface shall not justify disguising a supplier transaction as an employee expense.

5.5 Emergency expenses shall identify the event date, business need and reason prior purchasing procedures could not be followed. Receipt review, authority limits, classification and settlement requirements still apply. **Proposed deadline:** submit within one working day and obtain a spending decision within two working days. These are operating deadlines; the current code mainly gives emergency guidance and does not enforce a complete overdue escalation process.

5.6 Private expenditure, duplicate claims, unsupported personal benefits and purchases outside the Foundation's objects shall not be reimbursed. Conflicts of interest and purchases involving a director, employee or their related party shall be disclosed before approval. Accounts and the Company Secretary shall obtain any statutory approvals in addition to the internal spending decision.

## 6 Employee claims and supporting evidence

6.1 One accounting claim shall represent one invoice or bill. It may include several items from that bill. Different invoice dates shall use separate claims. The multi invoice form permits one submission containing 2 to 10 invoices, creates a linked claim for each invoice and preserves the combined approval basis. Each bill shall be reviewed, classified and settled as its own accounting record.

6.2 The employee shall enter the verified invoice date, supplier or payee, invoice number where available, item description, business purpose, amount, project spending label and actual funding source: personal money, own advance or permitted manager advance. Each bill shall have readable private evidence. A date or amount entered by the employee shall not override conflicting evidence on the original bill.

6.3 Evidence should show the supplier, date, goods or services, amounts and total; tax details where applicable; and proof that the employee paid where reimbursement is requested. Programme expenses should also have delivery, beneficiary, attendance or distribution evidence appropriate to the activity. Sensitive beneficiary details shall be restricted to people who need them.

6.4 The normal claim evidence formats are PDF, PNG and JPEG, with the portal's current file and submission size limits. Accounts shall retain the original electronic files and any original paper documents required for audit. A bank debit or UPI screenshot proves a payment but may not by itself explain what was purchased or support a tax credit.

6.5 The invoice preparation tool may document a genuine purchase where suitable supplier documentation is unavailable. It shall preserve the actual supplier identity, purchase date, items and amount. The employee or supplier signature shall clearly identify who made the declaration. Employee attestation shall be supported by payment and purchase evidence; it shall not be represented as the supplier's signature or automatically treated as a valid supplier tax invoice.

6.6 Signatures shall apply to the exact document content being signed. Changes to invoice or combined claim details after signing require fresh signatures. Generated document numbering follows its generation year and shall not be used to determine the financial year of the underlying expense. Evidence preparation alone shall not be treated as an accounting posting.

**Proposed ordinary deadline.** Submit bills within 15 calendar days of purchase or activity completion. Late submission requires an explanation and approval of the delay; it does not justify changing the invoice date. Accounts shall circulate a separate year end submission timetable and record any known unbilled liabilities. Genuine exceptions with incomplete evidence shall be returned for correction or handled through a documented exceptional accounting process; empty attachments or invented invoices shall never be accepted.

## 7 Receipt review approval and accounting finalisation

7.1 Submission enters Pending Receipt Review. The independent reviewer shall check readability, identity, dates, arithmetic, business evidence, payment source, duplicate indicators and relevant tax particulars. The reviewer shall Verify Receipts or Request Correction, with useful notes. Verification is an evidence decision and does not approve the business expense.

7.2 Changing an attachment before approval invalidates the reviewed snapshot and requires receipt review again. After approval, evidence changes shall follow cancellation and amendment. Accounts shall not reimburse a claim unless it is approved, receipt verification remains valid and the current files match the reviewed evidence. Older approvals shall not be assumed to certify previously unreviewed receipts.

7.3 After verification, the reporting authority shall assess necessity, project eligibility, price reasonableness, budget and the amount to sanction. A reduction or rejection shall include a reason. The manager may approve within authority, reject, or escalate one linked person at a time. A request to correct material facts shall return through the controlled process and any required renewed evidence review.

7.4 Manager approval moves the claim to Pending Accounts Classification and does not yet post the expense. An Accounts Manager shall allocate every sanctioned expense item to valid active leaf expense ledgers in the Foundation's chart. A single item may be split across several ledgers; positive allocations must exactly equal its sanctioned amount. Accounts shall not increase the manager's sanctioned amount through classification.

7.5 Accounts shall check the claim posting date, Project and Cost Centre, employee payable account, advance adjustment, possible duplicate posting and tax or asset treatment. If the bill requires an accounting treatment that the claim classifier cannot represent, Accounts shall use the authorised native accounting workflow and document the link, preventing duplicate posting through the pending claim.

7.6 Selecting Finalise Accounts submits the claim to accounting. The finalised claim creates the expense and the appropriate employee payable or advance adjustment. An unpaid claim remains a liability. Recording the bank reimbursement subsequently clears that liability. The Home reimbursement action settles the full outstanding bill; item by item bank reimbursement shall not be used.

| Stage | Ledger consequence for a personal expense | Budget consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Draft | No posting | No reservation |
| Submitted and receipt reviewed | No posting | Pending commitment reserved |
| Manager approves | No posting; awaits Accounts | Sanctioned commitment retained |
| Accounts finalises | Expense debit and employee liability credit | Posted claim remains in committed total |
| Bank payment is recorded | Employee liability debit and bank credit | No second expense or budget reservation |

## 8 Employee and team advances

8.1 An employee requesting an advance shall be active, eligible for an approved Active project and have Accounts Manager approved bank details. The request shall specify its intended project, purpose, amount, required by date, expected settlement date and whether it is for personal operational spending or team expenses. The intended project supports eligibility and planning; it does not itself book an expense or reserve project budget.

8.2 Any eligible employee may request any positive amount. Existing pending requests or unpaid balances do not automatically prohibit another request, and there is no general request count or outstanding balance cap in the current workflow. Approval authority shall nevertheless be assessed on live exposure under section 3. Accounts shall review overdue or legacy balances and liquidity before payment.

8.3 A reporting manager may freeze new advance requests for a direct report with a recorded reason. The freeze shall not prevent submission of bills, returns or reconciliation of an existing advance. Unfreeze decisions shall also retain their reason and history. **Proposed control:** overdue advances shall be reviewed weekly and the manager shall document whether a freeze or other recovery action is warranted.

8.4 Disbursement shall be recorded as an employee advance asset against the actual Bank or Cash ledger. Bills posted against that advance reduce the advance balance; they shall not create a second reimbursement for the same funded amount. If valid approved expenditure exceeds the available advance, only the remaining eligible personal expenditure shall be reimbursed through the appropriate settlement.

8.5 Unspent amounts shall be returned to the Foundation's authorised bank or cash custody and recorded against the original advance. Amounts shall remain assigned to the responsible employee until supported by bills, actual returns or an expressly authorised accounting adjustment. A small residual, including one below the portal's 10 percent manager source preference level, shall not be automatically written off.

8.6 Team advances shall be available only to employees with eligible direct reports and shall be marked Team expenses. The manager remains accountable for the float. Use of a manager advance shall identify the funded claim and evidence of delivery of money to the claimant. The portal may mark a manager funded claim reimbursed in the books at accounting finalisation; this shall be corroborated by the actual handover or transfer. Accounts shall not then issue a separate bank reimbursement for the same claim.

**Proposed settlement deadline.** Set the expected settlement date no later than seven calendar days after activity completion or 30 calendar days after disbursement, whichever is earlier, unless a longer project need is approved in writing. Complete bill submission and return of unspent money by that date. Recovery, payroll deduction and write off require separate authorised review under applicable law; they shall not be applied automatically because a balance is overdue.

## 9 Payments banking and cash custody

9.1 A Payment Entry records a payment; the current manual settlement actions do not initiate an external bank transfer. Bank release shall occur through the Foundation's authorised banking process. Accounts shall record only payments supported by successful transfer confirmation or actual cash handover and shall link the voucher to the approved claim, advance, order or invoice.

9.2 For an ordinary electronic payment, the Payment Entry posting date and transaction reference date shall be the actual bank transaction date supported by evidence. The date Accounts types the record remains its creation timestamp. A later bank value date shall be preserved during reconciliation. Cheque issuance, clearance and outstanding cheque differences shall be separately evidenced and reconciled under an approved cheque procedure.

9.3 Employee bank payments shall use the current approved reimbursement Bank Account. Employees submit their details and private proof; only Accounts Managers decide approval, return or rejection in the normal workflow. Pending replacements shall not change the existing approved destination. Once a replacement is approved, Accounts shall recheck any draft payment and use the new approved record.

9.4 Accounts shall compare holder name, account number and IFSC to proof and investigate mismatches before approval. Portal syntax checks shall not be treated as external bank verification. **Proposed control:** independently confirm changed destination details with the employee using a previously established contact method before the first payment. Supplier bank changes shall receive a similar documented verification.

9.5 The portal's default cash payment limit is INR 2,000 per payment. Digital payment shall be used above the saved limit. Related payments shall not be split to evade the limit. This internal outgoing payment limit is separate from donation receipt restrictions and tax deduction conditions.

9.6 A nominated cash custodian shall record cash receipts and payments promptly, obtain signed vouchers, secure cash and provide a count for reconciliation. Transfers between the Foundation's own cash and bank accounts are transfers of assets, not income or expenditure. Cash donations and employee cash returns shall receive separate acknowledgements identifying their nature.

**Proposed banking controls.** Use separate payment preparation and bank release authorisation for all electronic payments. Accounts shall record confirmed receipts and payments within two working days, reconcile every bank and gateway clearing account monthly, and obtain independent review. Unmatched entries, failed transfers, duplicate debits and unexplained balances shall be logged with an owner and follow up date.

## 10 Donations grants and restricted funds

10.1 Accounts shall record the donor, amount, actual receipt date, payment method, reference and receiving account. Noncash receipts require transaction evidence. Cheque donations shall be entered as successful receipts only after clearance under the Home donation workflow. Unidentified receipts shall be investigated and temporarily classified transparently rather than assigned an invented donor or issued an unsupported tax acknowledgement.

10.2 Home General donations require name, phone, address and PAN, and credit General Donations. Home CSR donations require an organisation name, address and PAN, and credit CSR Grants. Repeat donors shall be selected from the donor register. Corrections to donor masters shall preserve issued documents and transaction evidence.

10.3 General donations receive a numbered signed acknowledgement using an authorised Foundation signatory. For cash General donations the current form also requires the donor's signature. The CSR path does not generate the ordinary signed receipt. That presentation choice shall not remove any contractual acknowledgement, utilisation certificate, reconciliation or applicable statutory reporting obligation for CSR funds.

10.4 The ordinary receipt shall not be represented as the statutory donor deduction certificate. Accounts shall verify valid registration, eligibility and the reporting period before stating a tax benefit. For tax year 2026–27 onward, eligible donation reporting uses Form 113 and donor certificates use Form 114. Their normal deadline is 31 May following that tax year, subject to notified changes. Earlier periods shall use the applicable earlier forms. The portal's 80G labels shall be reconciled to current approval documents. [R2] [R3]

10.5 The cash deduction restriction currently checked by the portal when its 80G option is selected shall not be treated as a comprehensive cash receipt compliance control. Accounts shall separately check the applicable legal limits and donor eligibility; a receipt checkbox shall not determine the law or substitute for filing.

10.6 Cashfree receipts shall be accounted for only after verified successful payment, with linked gateway identifiers and accounting vouchers. Clearing is separate from the operating bank. The later settlement shall clear the gross receivable through the actual net bank receipt and separately recorded gateway fees and taxes. Accounts shall reconcile donation records, gateway successes, clearing entries and bank settlements; reprocessing a success shall not create another donation.

10.7 Corpus donations require an express donor direction and a separately approved accounting and investment treatment. Restricted grants require the agreement, approved purpose, eligible costs, reporting milestones, refund conditions and a fund movement register. Refundable or conditional receipts shall remain a liability until the applicable recognition conditions are met. Unconditional income subject to a use restriction shall retain that restriction in fund records. Amounts shall not be credited both to income and to a fund as two separate receipts.

10.8 Corpus, restricted and conditional receipts shall not be forced into the current two category Home donation form. Accounts shall use an approved accounting route and fund schedule after technical review by the Chartered Accountant. CSR eligibility and implementing agency registration shall be verified before accepting a CSR engagement. Foreign contributions shall be accepted only under valid FCRA authority and compliant banking, separately from domestic funds. The empty FCRA Accounts group in the chart does not establish that authority. [R4] [R5]

10.9 Donation refunds and chargebacks shall identify the original receipt, reason, authority and actual money movement. Accounts shall make the supported reversal or adjustment, retain the original record, and review whether an issued acknowledgement or statutory donation statement needs correction. Funds shall be refunded only to a verified destination under the authorised bank process.

## 11 Accounting classification and other balances

11.1 Accounts shall maintain one controlled Chart of Accounts. Create active posting ledgers only where an actual activity needs a distinct classification and use groups to organise them. Preserve useful existing ledgers and their links. The Foundation's chart separates Application of Funds, Source of Funds, Income and Expenses, including SF Axis Bank, Cashfree Clearing, Employee Advances, employee reimbursements and supplier payables.

11.2 Employee Reimbursements Payable shall be maintained separately from Supplier Payables. Historical claims that use an earlier payable ledger shall be reconciled until cleared. Renaming or moving an account shall not be treated as a transfer of balances, and no fund account shall be used interchangeably with a donation income account.

11.3 Accounts Managers shall control new ledgers, mapping changes and Company defaults. Record the reason and review existing references before disabling or reorganising accounts. An account with transactions shall be retained for historical reporting. Unclassified Employee Expenses is a technical preparation placeholder; it shall not become the final classification of a posted claim.

11.4 Durable items providing benefit over more than one reporting period shall be reviewed for capitalisation under the approved asset policy. **Proposed administrative threshold:** INR 10,000 per asset or inseparable functional set, subject to materiality and the applicable reporting framework. Do not split a functional asset into components to expense it. Low value items expensed shall still be tracked when custody matters. Accounts shall maintain asset cost, location, custodian, useful life, depreciation, disposal proceeds and impairment evidence; depreciation shall follow the approved statutory schedule, not a tax rate copied automatically.

11.5 Goods held for later distribution shall be recorded and periodically counted under an appropriate inventory or supplies procedure. Distribution evidence shall support programme costs. Physical loss, damage and write off require documented review. Donations in kind shall be recorded in a separate register with nature, quantity, restrictions and the approved valuation basis; they shall not be entered as fictitious bank receipts in Home.

11.6 Prepaid expenses shall be allocated over the benefit period. Known unpaid costs shall be accrued where appropriate; a later invoice shall be matched or the accrual reversed so expenditure is recorded once. Supplier and employee advances shall remain recoverable balances until valid settlement. Refunds, deposit recoveries and asset sales shall be classified according to the original transaction and relevant accounting treatment.

11.7 HR approved employee and payroll records shall support salary, deductions, employer contributions and net pay entries. Payroll advances and employee operational advances shall not be conflated. Accounts shall reconcile payroll liabilities and statutory dues monthly. GST input credits, output liabilities and withholding taxes shall use separate appropriate ledgers; charitable status shall not be assumed to remove these obligations. Recoverable tax shall be recognised only on eligible evidence and under the applicable rules.

## 12 Opening balances and historical transactions

12.1 The preferred migration basis shall be the complete verified opening position at the start of the selected financial year, followed by each actual transaction in that year. Earlier audited accounts shall remain preserved as prior year records. A bank opening balance alone shall not be treated as the complete balance sheet.

12.2 Accounts shall prepare a signed opening schedule covering each bank and cash account, outstanding employee advances, supplier balances, employee reimbursements, statutory dues, deposits, prepaid costs, assets and funds or accumulated balances. Party schedules shall reconcile to control accounts. Opening supplier invoices, advances and other subledger records shall be loaded through suitable opening documents wherever later settlement depends on them; their balances shall not also be loaded again in a general opening journal.

12.3 Home Starting balances records individual opening ledger and party amounts against Temporary Opening. Accounts shall attach available proof and retain the source reference. Temporary Opening shall be reconciled to zero when the full approved opening position has been recorded. Any provisional remainder shall have an identified cause, owner and resolution date. It shall not be reclassified to donations merely to remove the difference.

12.4 A prior year expense already included in audited accounts shall enter the new year's opening unpaid liability if still owed. Its new year reimbursement clears that opening liability; it shall not create the same expense again. Distinguish this from a genuine unrecorded prior period item requiring review under the approved financial reporting framework.

12.5 Historical entries shall preserve original invoice date, payment date, counterparty, reference, evidence and a unique source identifier. Accounts shall maintain an import register showing reviewed, posted, matched, rejected and duplicate items, plus voucher links. Classification shall distinguish donations, expenses, settlements, own account transfers, advances and returns. Reconcile opening balance plus all bank movements to the closing statement balance.

12.6 When starting with 1 April balances and adding April onward transactions later, retain the April opening entries and enter each movement once. If temporary balances were instead taken at a later cutover date, reverse or replace the overlapping bridge through controlled documents before or with backfill. Loading later balances and all earlier movements together without that reconciliation would double count them.

12.7 The Home fiscal year setup currently keeps years from 2024–25 active. Technical activation does not mean an audited or adopted year can be freely restated. During authorised reconstruction of genuinely open years, verified invoice dates may be used. Changes to approved prior year accounts require Accounts and Chartered Accountant review, and any necessary Board or statutory process.

12.8 Home supports opening balances but does not provide a complete historical bank import or unrestricted transaction entry workflow. Authorised Accounts staff shall use suitable native ERPNext documents for those transactions. Direct journals alone may not update Home claim, advance or budget counters; Accounts shall reconcile both operational balances and the General Ledger after migration.

## 13 Closing reporting and audit records

**Proposed monthly timetable.** By the fifth working day, Accounts shall complete transaction capture and obtain outstanding bill and advance information. By the tenth working day, Accounts shall complete bank and cash reconciliations, review party balances and circulate the monthly finance pack. An independent finance reviewer shall approve reconciliations and exceptions before the reporting period is marked closed in the agreed close register.

13.1 The finance pack shall include the Trial Balance, Balance Sheet, income and expenditure statement, cash and bank position, bank and clearing reconciliations, project budget commitments and ledger expenditure, unpaid supplier and employee liabilities, advance ageing, donor and restricted fund movements, and a list of corrections, overrides and unresolved exceptions.

13.2 Each balance shall have a named owner and supporting schedule. Review stale advances, unusual negative balances, unmatched bank receipts, clearing account ageing, temporary or suspense balances and expenses awaiting Accounts classification. Investigate rather than net unrelated balances or write them off without approval.

13.3 Year end work shall include confirmation of bank and material party balances, asset and inventory checks, unpaid cost and prepayment review, donation and grant restrictions, tax reconciliations and supporting disclosures. Accounts shall maintain a compliance calendar with responsible persons, due dates, applicable forms, filing evidence and follow up. The Chartered Accountant and Company Secretary shall confirm the tax year transition, applicable audit requirements and statutory filing obligations for the Foundation.

13.4 Monthly reporting closure and disabling a Fiscal Year are separate controls. Accounts shall maintain a close and reopening register; System Manager actions shall follow authorised instructions. A request to alter an adopted period shall specify the reason, affected vouchers, financial effect, reviewer and any corrected reports. The current Home fiscal year screen is a technical setup tool, not a complete financial close approval workflow.

13.5 Records shall include source bills, signed declarations, project approvals, receipt review history, classification splits, vouchers, bank evidence, advance returns, donor records, contracts, tax filings and reconciliations. Maintain records for at least the period required by law; for company books and supporting vouchers, preserve at least eight preceding financial years, or all preceding years where the company is younger. Preserve longer where an investigation, grant condition or unresolved matter requires it. [R1]

13.6 Private receipts, bank proof, PAN information and signatures shall be accessible only to authorised users. Downloads containing full bank details shall be protected. Shared logins and routine use of Administrator shall be prohibited. Accounts shall review financial access quarterly and revoke access promptly following role changes or departure.

**Proposed technology controls.** Preserve dated edit and transaction logs, take daily backups of database and private and public files, and test restoration quarterly. The technical owner and Chartered Accountant shall verify that the deployed logging, retention, accessibility and backup location meet applicable electronic books requirements. Ordinary workflow history and a successful backup job alone shall not be treated as a complete compliance assessment.

## 14 Exceptions and policy maintenance

14.1 Every exception shall record the transaction, amount, normal requirement, reason, supporting evidence, authorising person and follow up. Examples include emergency procurement, missing supplier documentation, delayed advance settlement, late submission and budget overruns. A policy exception shall not waive legislation, donor restrictions or mandatory evidence for a tax claim.

14.2 Suspected duplication, misuse of funds, altered evidence or an unexplained loss shall be referred promptly to the Accounts Manager and a Board designated director. Preserve records, restrict further affected payments as appropriate and document the investigation and resolution. Anyone involved in the transaction shall not be the sole reviewer of the concern.

14.3 Accounts shall review this policy annually with project leadership, HR, the technical owner and the Chartered Accountant. Maintain version, approval date, effective date and changes. Settings shall be amended only after authorisation, followed by a documented check of the revised workflow. Portal releases shall be checked against policy before operational adoption.

## Appendix C Accounting examples

These examples omit taxes and special accounting adjustments for clarity.

**Personal expense across financial years.** A valid bill is dated 28 March 2026, submitted on 5 April and reimbursed on 10 April. If the relevant prior year remains legitimately open and the expense was not previously recorded, Accounts finalises the claim with a 28 March posting date: debit the expense and credit Employee Reimbursements Payable. The 10 April Payment Entry debits that payable and credits Bank. If the cost was already in the prior year's adopted accounts, the April payment instead clears the verified opening liability.

**Advance with a return.** INR 10,000 is disbursed, INR 7,500 of eligible bills is posted and INR 2,500 is returned. Disbursement debits Employee Advances and credits Bank. The approved bill adjustment reduces the advance by INR 7,500 while recording the expense. The return debits Bank or Cash and credits Employee Advances by INR 2,500. The advance has no remaining recoverable balance and the expense is recorded only once.

**Gateway settlement with fees.** A INR 1,000 successful donation is recorded through Cashfree Clearing and donation income using the linked gateway vouchers. If the gateway settles INR 980 and retains INR 20 of charges, debit Bank INR 980 and the appropriate charges or tax ledgers totalling INR 20, and credit Cashfree Clearing INR 1,000. The settlement is not another donation.

**One submission with several invoices.** An employee submits three bills of INR 1,000 each. Each becomes its own dated accounting claim. The approval basis for the linked submission is at least INR 3,000, so a Manager with an INR 2,000 expense limit must escalate. The bills may be finalised and reimbursed separately after the applicable approvals, without lowering that approval basis.
